## 5. Privacy and Pupil Data

- **No accounts and no profiles.** The platform never requests a name, an email
  address, or any personal data whatsoever.
- **No data is sent to any server.** No analytics, no trackers, no third-party
  services.
- Learning progress is stored **locally on the device only**, in browser storage,
  under a namespace isolated per grade and semester: `waha:g{G}s{S}:`.
- On shared devices, entry offers **"Start a new session"** or **"Continue the
  last session on this device"**, so that one pupil never inherits another's
  progress.
- A teacher can clear device progress from within **Teacher Mode** using
  **"Clear this device's progress"**, with explicit confirmation; deletion is
  limited to the intended grade and semester.
- Progress remains on the device until cleared by one of the options above or by
  clearing browser data.
